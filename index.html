Space substitute

StandardThe ordinary space character, good for code, terminals and editors.
Most chat apps and social platforms drop leading or repeated spaces, so it is not recommended there.

No-breakA special space that looks as wide as a normal one and is less likely to be trimmed, but some major platforms turn it into a normal space, so check on your target platform.

BrailleThe blank braille character: the least likely to be trimmed, a good fit for chat and social platforms.
In proportional fonts its width may not line up, and code cannot treat it as a space.
